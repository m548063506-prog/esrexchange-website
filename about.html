About ESR Exchange

Built for serious IT resale, recovery, and recycling.

ESR Exchange helps companies, brokers, data centers, recyclers, and IT asset sellers move surplus technology equipment faster and safer.

Who we are

ESR Exchange was created to simplify the process of selling surplus IT equipment. Instead of listing every item one by one, sellers can upload a list and let ESR Exchange help organize the inventory by category, value, and buyer demand.

We focus on enterprise technology equipment including servers, networking gear, RAM, GPUs, storage, cables, telecom equipment, and electronic scrap.

What we do

  • Review inventory lists and equipment photos
  • Categorize parts by product type and demand
  • Support resale, recovery, and recycling options
  • Help sellers connect with qualified buyers
  • Support secure and brand-safe recovery processes

Why companies use ESR Exchange

Less manual work

Upload the list instead of creating hundreds of separate listings.

Better organization

Equipment is grouped by category so buyers can review it faster.

Recovery options

Items can be routed toward resale, parts recovery, or recycling.
